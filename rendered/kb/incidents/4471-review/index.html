Parser: month-first parsing of day-first input

The shared parser reads month first even when the tenant writes day first. For example, R-41200 (Harbour Dental, en-GB, app) describes 7 April becoming 4 July. App and email reports, and booking-v2 form reports, belong here for the day-first tenants in this export. Use the tenant directory, not the reporter’s browser locale.

Preserve the tenant’s intended day, month and year through parsing and invite generation. Check ambiguous dates in both directions, unambiguous dates, app/email/v2 paths, and month-first locales as controls. The parser draft excludes the five contradictory reports. Its remaining membership follows the supplied tenant/form/channel model and still needs production reproduction.

Hint: booking-v3 prints the wrong date convention

The v3 hint prints MM/DD/YYYY, while the form interprets input in the tenant’s day-first convention. Form submissions for Alder Physio (de-DE), Copper Studio (es-ES), and Pale Harbour (pt-PT) belong here. R-41230 describes 6 January becoming 1 June. These reports need their own ticket; changing the shared parser alone cannot repair this mismatch.

Generate the visible hint and form interpretation from the same tenant locale. Check all three affected tenants with an ambiguous date, v2 and app/email controls, and a month-first locale. Existing bookings need a separate review before any bulk correction.

Replace subject-only deduplication

This page proposes replacing decision 0001; the accepted historical record remains unchanged. Its June 2024 review date is stale under the KB’s review policy. The triage prose follows that decision, but the live explorer uses causes.ts and the tenant directory.

The same subject can describe different causes: Alder Physio’s form and app paths split. Different subjects and channels can share a cause: Kettle Books app and email reports merge. The same form subject can split across tenant form versions. Unknown tenants have no established cause and must remain unplaced until investigated, even when their subjects match.

Before merging, deduplicate export rows by report ID, read the body, look up the tenant’s locale and form, and compare the channel with the modeled cause. Treat contradictory symptoms as separate investigations instead of forcing them into that model. Merge only when evidence supports a shared cause. Preserve report IDs, tenant, channel, dates and body evidence. Do not merge merely because subjects or intake tags match, and do not label an investigation as a confirmed root cause.
